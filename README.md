## Fintech Credit Risk

**Python · scikit-learn · LightGBM · SHAP · Streamlit**

- Analyzed **307K+ customer records** across credit history, installments, prior applications, point-of-sale activity and card activity. Processed **240 features** for credit-default prediction.
- Compared classification models and evaluated the trade-off between precision and recall at different decision thresholds. **LightGBM achieved a validation ROC-AUC of 0.7833.**
- Used SHAP to explain individual predictions and built a Streamlit dashboard to explore credit-risk indicators.

## Revenue & Retention Analytics

**Python · pandas · SQL · DuckDB · matplotlib**

- Analyzed **18,491 observed orders** and **4,337 identified customers** in the UCI retail dataset.
- Separated revenue from repeat purchases from all revenue generated by returning customers. The second measure also includes their first orders.
- Compared retention across cohorts with enough follow-up time to observe a repeat purchase. Used RFM segmentation to group customers by recency, frequency and monetary value.

[Executive summary](https://github.com/moknihadil-eng/revenue-retention-analytics/blob/main/reports/executive-summary.md) · [SQL models](https://github.com/moknihadil-eng/revenue-retention-analytics/tree/main/sql) · [Methodology](https://github.com/moknihadil-eng/revenue-retention-analytics/blob/main/docs/methodology.md)
